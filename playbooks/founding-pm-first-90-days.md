# Founding PM: the first 90 days with pm-os

This is how I'd actually use this repo if I joined a seed or Series A
startup as the first product hire tomorrow. It's opinionated. Adjust it
to the company, but don't skip week one.

The principle: earn the right to change things by first writing down
what's true. Founders have usually been the product manager until now.
They don't need a PM who arrives with a process. They need one who
arrives, understands the product faster than anyone expected, and takes
work off their plate in the first month.

## Days 1 to 10: understand, write it down

| Do | Using |
|---|---|
| Founder interview, fill in `context/` | `pm-os-setup` |
| Talk to 5+ customers (sit in on sales and support calls if that's faster) | `customer-validation` interview guide |
| Read every ticket from the last month | `feedback-triage` |
| Log the decisions the founder would hate to re-argue | `decision-log` |
| Write down what the team calls things | `context/glossary.md` |

**What the founder sees by day 10:** a five-line playback of their
product that they agree with, a short list of open questions, and a
feedback summary they didn't have to read 300 tickets to get.

## Days 11 to 30: set the rhythm, take something off their plate

| Do | Using |
|---|---|
| Agree the north star and the metric tree | `context/metrics.md` |
| Start the weekly product review | `weekly-product-review` |
| Pick the biggest open question and run discovery on it | `discovery` |
| Take over one thing the founder is still doing (usually specs or the roadmap) | `prd-generation` or `prioritisation-and-roadmap` |
| Run a review on whatever doc the team is building from today | `product-review` |

**What the founder sees by day 30:** a weekly meeting that's shorter
than the one it replaced, a metric tree people argue about (good), and
one fewer job on their plate.

## Days 31 to 60: first real bet

| Do | Using |
|---|---|
| Size the top opportunity | `market-sizing` |
| Test the riskiest assumption before building | `customer-validation`, `prototyping` |
| Write the PRD with engineering in the room | `prd-generation` |
| Re-plan Now / Next / Later around it | `prioritisation-and-roadmap` |
| Start a fortnightly founder or investor update | `stakeholder-update` |

## Days 61 to 90: ship, measure, write the strategy

| Do | Using |
|---|---|
| Launch the first bet properly | `launch-readiness` |
| Read the result honestly | `experimentation` |
| Write a one-page product strategy, grounded in what's now in `context/` | `product-strategy` |
| Hand `context/` to the next engineer who joins, and see if they get it | (the real test) |

**What the founder sees by day 90:** one shipped, measured bet, a
strategy written from evidence rather than the pitch deck, and a product
function that would keep running if the PM went on holiday.

## Signs it's not working
- `context/` hasn't been updated in three weeks. The weekly review has
  quietly stopped.
- The founder is still writing specs. You haven't taken anything off
  them yet.
- Every bet is "high confidence". Nobody's being honest about risk.
- The decision log is empty. Decisions are still being made in DMs.
