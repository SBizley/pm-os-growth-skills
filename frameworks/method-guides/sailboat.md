# Sailboat

*Goals · 1 hr · Alignment, Kickoff, Problem-Solving*

## Purpose

Use this to clarify what your team is trying to achieve, what is driving you forward, and what is holding you back. The visual metaphor makes it easy to surface and prioritise goals, motivators, and blockers.

## When to Use

- At the beginning of a project or initiative
- When the team feels unclear about what success looks like
- When you need alignment on what's stopping progress
- In retrospectives to identify blockers

## How to Run

**Duration:** 1 hour

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes, markers

**Steps:**

1. On a whiteboard, draw a boat out at sea. Explain that this represents your team or project.
2. **Draw an island** ahead of the boat. This represents your shared goal. Ask everyone to write down what they think the team's goals are (one per sticky note).
3. One by one, have participants share their goals and place them near the island. Group similar ones together.
4. **Draw a sail** on the boat. Explain this represents what is driving you forward. Ask the group to write drivers on sticky notes (e.g., motivation, resources, market demand). Place them near the sail.
5. **Draw an anchor** below the boat. Explain this represents the barriers or blockers. Ask the group to write down what is slowing progress and stick them near the anchor.
6. Discuss the notes briefly as they go up, grouping them into themes.
7. When the map feels complete, hold a secret vote: first on the most important goals, then on the most critical blockers.
8. Capture the top-ranked items and agree how they will guide your next steps.

## Tips

- The metaphor helps participants express thoughts more openly than a blank list
- Use this as a warm-up for tactics like Sticky Steps or Problem Statement
- Take a photo of the final sailboat for future reference
