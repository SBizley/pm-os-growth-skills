# Idea Storm

*Recipe · 3-4 hrs · Ideation, Problem-Solving*

## Purpose

Generate a wide range of solutions to a problem, then refine and evaluate them into a shortlist of strong ideas.

## When to Use

- After framing a problem clearly
- When the team feels stuck with too few ideas
- To break through creative blocks

## How to Run

**Duration:** 3-4 hours

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes, markers, paper for sketching

**Steps:**

1. **How Might We… (1 hr)** - Reframe the problem into open-ended questions.
2. **Mind Map (30 min)** - Warm up creativity by mapping associations around the question.
3. **Idea Eight (35 min)** - Push for eight ideas in eight minutes per person.
4. **T-Bar Format (1 hr)** - Develop ideas into a sketch plus description.
5. **Rose, Thorn, Bud (30 min)** - Evaluate positives, negatives, and opportunities.

## Tips

- Emphasise quantity before quality - go wide before going deep
- Works well as a bridge into prioritisation (e.g., Impact/Effort Map)
- Keep energy high with breaks and time pressure

## Outcome

A shortlist of promising, clearly expressed, and stress-tested ideas.
