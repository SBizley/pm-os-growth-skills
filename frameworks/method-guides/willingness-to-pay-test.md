# Willingness-to-Pay Test

*Evaluate · 2 days · Validation Solution*

# **Purpose**

Find the price range customers perceive as fair vs too high/low.

# **When to Use**

- Before finalising pricing
- To test monetisation potential early

# **How to Run**

**Duration:** 1–2 days

1. Ask the four **Van Westendorp** questions (too cheap, cheap, expensive, too expensive).
2. Plot intersections to reveal acceptable range.
3. Validate with interviews or prototype purchase screens.

# **Example**

SME survey (n = 60) → acceptable range £9–£15/month.

# **Tips**

- Phrase in value language, not budget.
- Compare across customer segments.
