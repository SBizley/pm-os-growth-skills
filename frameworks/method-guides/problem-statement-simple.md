# Problem Statement - Simple

*Frame · 1 hr · Alignment, Just starting, Kickoff, Problem-Solving*

## Purpose

Bring clarity and focus by capturing the project's goals, the unmet needs, and the constraints in a single problem statement. It ensures alignment and prevents people from jumping straight to solutions.

## When to Use

- At the start of a project, after initial exploration
- When there's disagreement on what the "real problem" is
- As preparation for discovery and ideation workshops

## How to Run

**Duration:** 1 hour

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes

**Steps:**

1. Gather relevant stakeholders.
2. Ask the group to answer these prompts on sticky notes:
    - What are the current goals of the product or service?
    - What problem do stakeholders want addressed?
    - Which goals aren't being met?
    - What's the request for improvement (without naming a solution)?
3. Group the notes into themes.
4. Draft a shared problem statement using this template:

> "[Our product/service] was designed to achieve [these goals].
> 

> We have observed that it isn't meeting [these goals], which is causing [this negative effect].
> 

> How might we improve [product/service] so that our customers are more successful based on [these measurable criteria]?"
> 
1. Refine as a group until there's consensus.

## Tips

- Keep it short - one paragraph max
- Use this to anchor subsequent discovery and ideation work
- Revisit and refine as you learn more
