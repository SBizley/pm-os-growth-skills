# Who/What/When

## Purpose

Ensure workshops end with clear commitments, not just discussion. Captures ownership, specific actions, and deadlines.

## When to Use

- At the end of any workshop
- When you need accountability for follow-up actions
- After any discussion that needs to result in concrete next steps

## How to Run

**Duration:** 10 minutes

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace

**Steps:**

1. Draw three columns: Who, What, When.
2. List each participant under "Who."
3. Ask each person to state what concrete action they can commit to. Write it under "What."
4. For each action, agree a deadline and write it under "When."
5. Review the list as a group and confirm commitments.

## Tips

- Push for specific dates, not vague timelines ("next week" isn't enough)
- Encourage quieter participants to take at least one action
- Capture and share the list afterwards to maintain accountability
- Make actions specific and achievable
