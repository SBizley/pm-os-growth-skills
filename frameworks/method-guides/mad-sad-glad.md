# Mad/Sad/Glad

## Purpose

Give the team space to reflect emotionally on recent work. Understanding feelings helps address underlying issues and build trust.

## When to Use

- In regular retrospectives
- After a stressful sprint, project, or event
- When morale feels low or uneven

## How to Run

**Duration:** 1 hour

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes

**Steps:**

1. Draw three columns: **Mad, Sad, Glad**.
2. Ask participants to write down what made them mad, sad, and glad (one per sticky note).
3. Share notes aloud and cluster them into themes.
4. Discuss as a group.
5. Prioritise top issues with a Secret Vote.
6. Turn outcomes into concrete actions.

## Tips

- Create a safe environment by stressing there are no "wrong" feelings
- Use this to identify emotional patterns that might otherwise be ignored
- Balance time between processing feelings and planning actions
