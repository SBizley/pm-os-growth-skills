# Rose, Thorn, Bud

*Evaluate · 30 min · Problem-Solving, Retro*

## Purpose

Evaluate an idea, project, or process by identifying positives (roses), negatives (thorns), and opportunities (buds).

## When to Use

- To quickly test ideas as a group
- Mid-project to reflect on what's working and what's not
- In retrospectives for balanced feedback

## How to Run

**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes (red, yellow, green)

**Steps:**

1. Select the topic to evaluate (e.g., a design, project, idea, or even a process).
2. Explain the colour coding:
    - **Red** = Roses (positives)
    - **Yellow** = Thorns (negatives)
    - **Green** = Buds (opportunities)
3. Ask everyone to write notes under each category.
4. Cluster and review the themes together.
5. Discuss actions to address thorns and cultivate buds.

## Tips

- This method works well in both retros and ideation sessions
- Keep the pace brisk: it's designed as a quick group pulse
- Balance focus between celebrating roses and addressing thorns
