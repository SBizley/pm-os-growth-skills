# Business Model Road Test

*Evaluate · 2 days · Validation Solution*

*Adapted from John W. Mullins, The New Business Road Test (FT Prentice Hall, 2003)*

# **Purpose**

Assess whether your **product, market, and team** form an attractive, scalable business model - before committing serious investment.

This workshop helps identify strengths, weaknesses, and evidence gaps across **market**, **industry**, and **team** domains, so you can design follow-up validation experiments.

# **When to Use**

- When exploring a **new market, product line, or pricing model**
- Before delivery inception or investment sign-off
- To re-evaluate a business model post-launch or pivot

# **How to Run**

**Duration:** ½ – 1 day  

**Materials:** Business Model Canvas · Seven Domains template · existing evidence

1. **Frame the opportunity** - summarise the validated problem, target segment, and early traction.
2. **Map evidence into each domain**
    - Market (macro + micro)
    - Industry (macro + micro)
    - Team (mission, execution ability, connectedness)
3. **Score confidence 1-5** for each domain
    - 1 = assumption only 5 = validated with real data
4. **Highlight red zones** (≤ 3) and define next validation tests – pricing, channel, unit economics, ops pilot.
5. **Summarise Go / Hold / Pivot** decision and update your validation backlog.

### **Domains & Questions**

| Domain | Focus | Key Questions to Answer | Typical Validation Techniques |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Market Macro** | Market size & growth | Is the total addressable market large and growing? | TAM/SAM/SOM analysis, trend & search data |
| **Market Micro** | Target segment benefits | Do specific customers care enough to switch or pay? | Customer interviews, JTBD, WTP, Conjoint |
| **Industry Macro** | Structural attractiveness | Is this an industry where participants can profit? | Porter 5 Forces, regulation scan |
| **Industry Micro** | Sustainable advantage | Can we defend our edge against competitors? | Retention analysis, IP audit, moat map |
| **Team Mission & Risk** | Goal alignment & risk fit | Is this opportunity consistent with our ambition and risk profile? | Alignment workshop, risk map |
| **Team Execution Ability** | CSFs & capability | Do we have the skills and resources that matter most? | Capability matrix, skills audit |
| **Team Connectedness** | Partnership & distribution | Are we plugged into key partners and channels? | Channel pilot, partner interviews |

> *“Good opportunities can be found in not-so-attractive industries - if the micro-level market and industry domains look strong.” - Mullins*
> 

### **Example**

| Domain | Confidence (1-5) | Learning | Next Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Market Micro | 4 | Strong merchant demand for Instant Payouts | Conjoint pricing test |
| Industry Macro | 3 | Crowded but fast-growing payments space | Benchmark fees |
| Team Connectedness | 2 | Limited EU partners | Run 4-week channel pilot |

### **Outputs**

✅ Business Model Health Map with confidence scores

✅ Targeted validation backlog to close red zones

✅ Shared viability rating before build or scale

### **Tips**

- Treat this as a **pre-mortem:** ask *“Why will or won’t this work?”*
- Weight **Industry** and **Team** evidence as heavily as **Market**.
- Look for behavioural proof (money spent, time invested) over opinions.
- Re-run after each major pivot or market expansion.
