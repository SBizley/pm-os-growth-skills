# Story Mapping

*Decide · 2-3 hrs · Ready to decide, Prioritisation · PM, design, 2+ engineers, and whoever talks to customers*

## Purpose

Lay the customer's journey out left to right, stack what they do underneath each step, then draw a line across it: everything above the line is v1. It's how I turn "we've validated this" into a scope the whole team agrees to, and it's the session where engineers most often save us from ourselves.

## When I reach for it

- After validation, before anyone writes a ticket
- When a PRD's got too big and nobody can say what the smallest useful version is

## How I run it

1. **One user, one goal, one metric (10 min).** Write them at the top. If there are two personas, run two maps.
2. **The backbone (20 min).** The 5 to 7 big steps the customer takes, in their words, left to right. Example (fictional studio booking app): find a class → book → pay → get reminded → attend → rebook.
3. **What they do under each step (40 min).** Sticky notes stacked vertically, most essential at the top. Engineers add what the system has to do.
4. **Mark the risk (15 min).** Flag anything that's an unvalidated assumption, a dependency on another team or partner, or a known pain from research.
5. **Draw the line (30 min).** Everything above it ships in v1. The test: can a customer get from the first step to the last, even if it's ugly? A thin slice end to end beats a polished first half.
6. **Name the metric for the slice (10 min).** What will tell us v1 worked? Which event do we need tracked on day one?
7. **Owners and next steps (10 min).** The slice becomes epics. Photograph the map and put it next to the PRD.

## Where it goes wrong

- It turns into backlog grooming, with people writing tickets instead of mapping a journey
- The line gets drawn by what's easy to build, not what makes the journey work end to end
- The backbone uses internal system names instead of what the customer is actually doing

## What you walk out with

A v1 slice everyone has seen drawn, the risks on it, and the metric that tells you it worked. It goes straight into the PRD Generation skill.
