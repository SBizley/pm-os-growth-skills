# Premortem

*Evaluate · 1 hr · Kickoff, Problem-Solving, Ready to decide*

## Purpose

Anticipate failure before it happens. Instead of waiting for things to go wrong, you imagine that the project has already failed spectacularly and ask "why?"

## When to Use

- At the start of a project, after initial planning
- When you want to identify risks and mitigation strategies early
- Before committing significant resources

## How to Run

**Duration:** 1 hour

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes

**Steps:**

1. Present the current plan or roadmap.
2. Ask the group to imagine it is the future and the project has failed completely.
3. Each participant writes down reasons for failure (one per sticky note).
4. Share notes and briefly explain each one.
5. Cluster similar causes into themes.
6. Place themes onto an Assumption Map (e.g., certain vs uncertain, high vs low risk).
7. Develop actions to mitigate or investigate the riskiest reasons.
8. Capture ownership with Who/What/When.

## Tips

- Encourage people to be brutally honest
- Frame as a learning exercise, not pessimism
- This surfaces concerns people might be afraid to voice otherwise
