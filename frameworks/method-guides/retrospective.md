# Retrospective

*Recipe · 2 hrs · Alignment, Retro*

## Purpose

Reflect on recent work to celebrate successes, identify issues, and agree on improvements for next time.

## When to Use

- Regularly (e.g., every 2 weeks in agile teams)
- At the end of a project or release
- When the team needs to learn from recent experiences

## How to Run

**Duration:** 2 hours

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes, markers

**Steps:**

1. **Choose a format (1 hr):**
    - **Mad / Sad / Glad** – focus on emotions
    - **Three Little Pigs** – focus on robustness and risk
    - **Start / Stop / Continue** – focus on actions
2. **Run the activity:**
    - Have everyone contribute sticky notes under each category
    - Share and cluster similar themes
    - Discuss patterns and insights
3. **Democratic Discussion (30 min)** – Prioritise the most important themes.
4. **Who, What, When (30 min)** – Turn outcomes into concrete next steps.

## Tips

- Keep retros safe and constructive: avoid blame
- Always close with 2-3 clear actions, not just reflections
- Rotate the format to keep retros fresh
- Celebrate wins before diving into problems

## Outcome

The team learns, adapts, and grows with each cycle.
