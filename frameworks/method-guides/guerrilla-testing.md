# Guerrilla Testing

*Evaluate · 1 hr · Validation Solution*

# **Purpose**

Quickly spot major usability issues with minimal setup.

# **When to Use**

- Early prototype phase
- To sanity-check flows or language

# **How to Run**

**Duration:** 1–2 hrs

1. Approach 5–10 participants in a public or office setting.
2. Ask them to complete one task on the prototype.
3. Note success, confusion, time to first click.

# **Example**

5 users → 3 missed “Next Step” button → UI label change validated.

# **Tips**

- Keep sessions < 10 min.
- Ideal for copy or navigation checks.
