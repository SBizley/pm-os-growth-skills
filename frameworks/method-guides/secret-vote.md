# Secret Vote

*Techniques · 5 min · Prioritisation*

## Purpose

Make quick, democratic decisions while avoiding bias or dominant voices.

## When to Use

- When the group needs to pick from multiple options
- To prevent "bandwagon" effects in discussions
- For fair, unbiased decision-making

## How to Run

**Duration:** 5 minutes

**Materials needed:** Paper, pens (or digital polling tool)

**Steps:**

1. Number the items up for decision (1, 2, 3, …).
2. Explain each item briefly.
3. Give each participant three votes they can distribute however they like (all on one item, or spread out).
4. Ask them to write their votes privately.
5. Collect and tally results. Display the outcome clearly.

## Tips

- Works best when paired with T-Bar Format or storyboards so people understand what they're voting on
- Keep it secret until reveal — that's what prevents bias
- Can use dot voting as a visible alternative, but lose the anti-bias benefit
