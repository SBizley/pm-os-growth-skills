# Proposition Testing

*Evaluate · 2 days · Validation Solution*

# **Purpose**

Identify which value proposition resonates most with your target audience.

# **When to Use**

- Before launch or campaign creative
- To test positioning variants

# **How to Run**

**Duration:** 1 day

1. Write 3–5 headline + subtext propositions.
2. Test via ads (A/B) or survey preference.
3. Track CTR / recall / qual feedback.

# **Example**

“Get Paid Instantly” → 3× CTR vs “Faster Payouts.”

# **Tips**

- Keep visuals identical; only vary messaging.
- Combine with Fake Door for behavioural data.
