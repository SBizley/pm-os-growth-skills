# Hypothesis Statement

*Frame · 30 min · Discovery, Problem-Solving, Ready to decide*

## Purpose

Turn assumptions into testable experiments. Helps teams avoid political debates by grounding decisions in evidence.

## When to Use

- After mapping assumptions (e.g., with Assumption Map)
- When planning experiments or MVPs
- To focus discovery on the riskiest unknowns

## How to Run

**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes

**Steps:**

1. Collect the riskiest assumptions.
2. Reframe them using this template:

> Because we saw [insight], we believe [assumption is true].
> 

> 
> 

> We will know we are right when we see [feedback/metric/KPI].
> 
1. If the assumption is large, break it into smaller hypotheses using:

> We believe that [doing X] for [these people] will achieve [this outcome].
> 

> 
> 

> We will know this is true when we see [feedback/metric].
> 
1. Hold a secret vote on which hypotheses to test first.

## Tips

- Keep each hypothesis testable within a reasonable timeframe
- Use qualitative (user feedback) and quantitative (metrics) indicators together
- Revisit and update as you gather evidence
