# Impact/Effort Map

## Purpose

Quickly sort ideas by how much impact they deliver vs how much effort they require. Helps prioritise into "do now," "do later," or "forget it."

## When to Use

- After idea generation when you need to shortlist
- When there are too many potential directions
- To focus team energy on high-value work

## How to Run

**Duration:** 30-60 minutes

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes

**Steps:**

1. Draw a grid with Impact (high to low) on one axis and Effort (high to low) on the other.
2. Write each idea on a sticky note.
3. As a group, discuss and plot them on the grid:
    - High impact, low effort → "Do now"
    - High impact, high effort → "Do later"
    - Low impact, low effort → "Maybe later / nice-to-have"
    - Low impact, high effort → "Forget it"
4. Create a plan for "Do now" items using Sticky Steps.

## Tips

- Encourage honesty about effort levels: teams tend to underestimate
- Works well paired with Secret Vote if there are many ideas
- Revisit regularly as priorities shift
