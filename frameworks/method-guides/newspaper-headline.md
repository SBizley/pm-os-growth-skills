# Newspaper Headline

*Goals · 30 min · Kickoff, Ready to decide, Team Building*

## Purpose

Encourage the team to imagine a future in which the project or organisation has been a big success - and summarise it as if it were on the front page of a newspaper. This draws out ambition and reveals what people truly value.

## When to Use

- To create an inspiring shared vision
- At the start of a new project or initiative
- When the team needs energizing around a common goal

## How to Run

**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Materials needed:** Blank paper, markers

**Steps:**

1. Ask everyone to sketch a simple newspaper front page on a blank sheet. Include sections for:
    - **Headline** – a punchy one-line summary of extraordinary success
    - **Subheading** – a supporting detail
    - **Sketch** – a simple drawing that captures the story
    - **Report** – bullet points describing highlights of the success
    - **Quotes** – imaginary statements from customers, stakeholders, or the press
2. Give the group 10-15 minutes to create their individual "future headlines."
3. Go around the group and have each person share their page.
4. Discuss common themes and combine them into one or two overarching visions.

## Tips

- Works best if people are encouraged to think big, not just incremental improvements
- Can be followed by Sailboat or OKRs to turn the vision into concrete steps
- Creates a fun, creative atmosphere at the start of workshops
