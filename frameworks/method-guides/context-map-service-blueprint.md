# Context Map / Service Blueprint

# **Purpose**

Visualise the ecosystem surrounding your problem — who’s involved, what systems interact, and where friction emerges.

# **When to Use**

- Early in discovery to clarify the environment and stakeholders
- When problems cross multiple teams or processes
- To uncover hidden dependencies or ownership gaps

# **How to Run**

**Duration:** 1–1.5 hours

**Materials:** Miro or large whiteboard

# **Steps:**

1. Place your **customer or product** at the centre.
2. Add surrounding systems, tools, or people who interact with it.
3. Map connections (data, communication, dependencies).
4. Annotate friction points or breakdowns.
5. Summarise “tensions” and “opportunities” observed.

# **Example**

A “Failed payout” problem involves Customer Support, Banking APIs, and Finance Ops - mapping them revealed a dependency on manual reconciliation.

# **Tips**

- Keep one map per key process.
- Use arrows to show dependency direction.
- Capture both blockers and enablers.
