# Value Proposition Exercise

*Evaluate · 90 min workshop, then a few days of testing · Validation Solution · PM, design, marketing or sales, a founder if you can get one*

## Purpose

Two parts. In the room, map one customer segment's jobs, pains and gains against what we actually offer, and write a fit statement we'd be willing to defend. Outside the room, test two or three versions of that statement with real people.

## When I reach for it

- A new product or segment, before any marketing copy gets written
- Sales and product describe the product differently
- Activation is low and we suspect people don't get what it's for

## How I run it

1. **One segment (5 min).** Name exactly who. "SMBs" isn't a segment. "Owner-run studios with 3 to 5 instructors" is.
2. **Jobs, pains, gains (30 min).** Customer side first, from research, not opinion. Every sticky needs a source: a quote, a ticket, a data point. Rank the pains by how badly they hurt.
3. **What we offer (20 min).** Product side. Each pain reliever has to point at a specific ranked pain. Anything that doesn't map to a pain gets put to one side. That pile is usually revealing.
4. **Write the fit statement (15 min).** For [segment] who need to [top job], [product] helps by [top pain reliever], unlike [alternative], because [evidence]. Write two or three competing versions.
5. **Plan the test (20 min).** Pick the cheapest real-world test: a landing page with each version, a few ads, a short survey, or reading the statements out on five customer calls. Decide up front what result would make you pick one.
6. **Run it (a few days).** Keep everything identical except the words. Look at what people do (clicks, sign-ups) over what they say they prefer.

Example (fictional): "Fill every class spot" against "Never chase a no-show again" for a studio booking app. Same page, same design, only the headline changes.

## Where it goes wrong

- The customer side gets filled in from the team's assumptions, so the canvas just reflects what we already believed
- Pain relievers turn into a features list
- Testing with existing fans, who'll like anything

## What you walk out with

A fit statement backed by evidence, and the one that won a real test. Use `templates/value-proposition-canvas-template.md` for the canvas itself.
