# Concierge Test

*Evaluate · 2 days · Validation Solution*

# **Purpose**

Offer the experience manually to learn what customers truly value and would pay for.

# **When to Use**

- When automation is costly or uncertain
- To validate willingness to pay and repeat use

# **How to Run**

**Duration:** 1–2 weeks

1. Deliver the outcome manually (spreadsheet, DM, email).
2. Observe behaviour, record effort, and capture quotes.
3. Ask what would make them continue if automated.

# **Example**

Ops team manually sent daily payout reports to 10 merchants; 8 continued for 2 weeks → validated product concept.

# **Tips**

- Keep sample small (5–15 users).
- Track retention and effort cost.
