# SWOT Analysis

*Evaluate · 1-2 hrs · Alignment, Problem-Solving, Understanding the problem*

## Purpose

Assess the Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats related to a project, idea, or strategy. Gives a balanced view of current position.

## When to Use

- To evaluate a new idea, team, or plan
- When comparing options or approaches
- At strategic planning sessions

## How to Run

**Duration:** 1-2 hours

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes

**Steps:**

1. Share the desired end-state or objective (e.g., from OKRs or Newspaper Headline).
2. Draw a quadrant with four sections: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats.
3. Ask participants to fill each quadrant with sticky notes.
4. Cluster themes and label them.
5. Vote on the most important factors in each quadrant.
6. Discuss implications and generate "How Might We…" questions to address them.

## Tips

- Keep focus on the specific goal, not a generic SWOT
- Use it to generate next steps rather than just a static analysis
- Internal factors = Strengths/Weaknesses; External factors = Opportunities/Threats
