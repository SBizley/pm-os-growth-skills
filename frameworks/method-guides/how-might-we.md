# How Might We

*Frame · 1 hr · Ideation, Just starting, Problem-Solving*

## Purpose

Transform problems into open-ended, solution-oriented questions. Instead of saying "customers don't use our feature," reframe to "How might we encourage customers to try this feature?" This shifts the team into solution mode.

## When to Use

- After research or discovery sessions when problems have been surfaced
- Before idea generation, as a bridge to creativity
- To ensure problems are framed broadly enough to allow multiple solutions

## How to Run

**Duration:** 1 hour

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes

**Steps:**

1. Present the group with the problem area (this could be findings from research, stakeholder interviews, or a Journey Map).
2. Explain how to reframe:
    - Listen for problems
    - Recast them as "How might we…" questions
    - Example: Problem → "Customers don't contact us when they have an issue." → HMW → "How might we make it easier for customers to contact us when they have an issue?"
3. Ask participants to write as many HMW questions as they can on sticky notes.
4. Share, group similar ones, and discuss.
5. Prioritise with a quick dot vote.

## Tips

- Make sure the phrasing allows for multiple answers (avoid overly narrow wording)
- Use HMWs directly as prompts for idea-generation tactics like Crazy 8s
- Frame questions optimistically - focus on opportunities, not just fixing problems
