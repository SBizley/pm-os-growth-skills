# Mind Map

*Ideas · 30 min · Ideation, Problem-Solving*

## Purpose

Unleash free association by mapping ideas visually. This warms up creative thinking and generates unexpected connections.

## When to Use

- At the start of ideation to get everyone's brains moving
- When tackling a broad or fuzzy problem
- As a creative warm-up before deeper exercises

## How to Run

**Duration:** 30 minutes

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or paper, markers

**Steps:**

1. Write the topic or challenge in the centre of the page (use a How Might We question if you have one).
2. Give participants 5 minutes to capture anything related that pops into their minds - each idea on its own branch radiating out.
3. After the first wave, ask them to expand on branches with related thoughts. Connect with lines.
4. Encourage drawing, symbols, or colour coding.
5. Optionally, repeat with a new central branch or theme that emerged.

## Tips

- The goal is **quantity, not quality** - let it flow
- Use it as direct stimulus for Idea Eight or Round Robin
- Don't judge ideas during the mapping phase
