# Project Kick-Off

*Recipe · 4 hrs · Discovery, Kickoff, Problem-Solving*

## Purpose

Align the team, clarify the problem, and surface assumptions and risks before starting a project.

## When to Use

- At the very beginning of a new project
- When multiple stakeholders need to be aligned fast
- Before committing resources to a major initiative

## How to Run

**Duration:** 4 hours

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes, markers

**Steps:**

1. **Premortem (1 hr)** – Pretend the project failed. Write down reasons why.
2. **Problem Statement (1 hr)** – Agree on the problem and how you'll know success.
3. **Assumption Collecting (45 min)** – Surface what the team believes about the user, product, and business.
4. **Assumption Map (45 min)** – Plot assumptions by risk and certainty to find the riskiest unknowns.
5. **Hypothesis Statement (30 min)** – Frame key assumptions as experiments to test.

## Tips

- Make sure all key stakeholders are present — otherwise hidden risks stay hidden
- Revisit the Hypotheses after your first research sprint
- Document everything and share immediately after the session

## Outcome

A clear project focus, aligned team, and a roadmap for testing assumptions.
