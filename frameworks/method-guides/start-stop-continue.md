# Start/Stop/Continue

## Purpose

Encourage the team to reflect on behaviours or practices that are helpful, harmful, or worth keeping. A simple, balanced format that always leads to clear actions.

## When to Use

- Midway through or at the end of a project
- In retrospectives or feedback sessions
- When a team feels stuck in unproductive habits

## How to Run

**Duration:** 1 hour

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes

**Steps:**

1. Draw three columns on a board: **Start, Stop, Continue**.
2. Ask each person to write sticky notes for each column:
    - What should we start doing?
    - What should we stop doing?
    - What should we continue doing?
3. Share and stick notes up, grouping similar ones.
4. Discuss key themes as a group.
5. Prioritise with a Secret Vote if needed.
6. Capture commitments using Who/What/When.

## Tips

- Keep the focus on actions, not complaints
- Works equally well for team processes or personal feedback
- Balance criticism (Stop) with positivity (Continue)
