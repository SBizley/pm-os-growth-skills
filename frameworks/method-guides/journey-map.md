# Journey Map

*Understand · 1-2 hrs · Discovery, Problem-Solving, Understanding the problem*

## Purpose

Map the customer's journey to identify moments of delight and frustration. Helps highlight opportunities for improvement across the full experience.

## When to Use

- Early in discovery
- When you need a shared view of the end-to-end customer experience
- To identify specific pain points

## How to Run

**Duration:** 1-2 hours

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes, markers

**Steps:**

1. Define the start and end of the journey (e.g., "visiting pizza website" to "eating pizza").
2. Ask participants to capture each step in between on sticky notes.
3. Place the steps along a timeline in order.
4. For each step, decide as a group whether it's delightful, neutral, or frustrating. Mark accordingly (e.g., green for positive, red for negative).
5. For the frustrating steps, reframe them into "How Might We…" questions.
6. Prioritise which pain points to address first.

## Tips

- Keep the journey simple - it's about customer perception, not internal processes
- Use follow-up tactics like Impact/Effort Map to prioritise which improvements to tackle
- Validate with real customer research, not just assumptions
