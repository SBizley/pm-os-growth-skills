# Assumption Reversal

*Ideas · 45 min · Ideation*

# **Purpose**

Flip your thinking by questioning the assumptions that constrain current solutions.

**When to Use**

- When ideas feel repetitive or conservative
- To uncover breakthrough or contrarian concepts

# **How to Run**

**Duration:** 45 mins

**Materials:** Whiteboard, sticky notes

# **Steps:**

1. List your current assumptions about the problem or user behaviour.
2. Reverse them - imagine the opposite were true.
3. Generate ideas that could exist in that world.
4. Identify any that are surprisingly feasible.

# **Example**

Assumption: “Merchants need to log in to check payouts.”

Reversal: “Merchants never log in.”

New idea: “Payout updates delivered via WhatsApp or Slack.”

# **Tips**

- Encourage wild reversals - they spark creativity.
- Use with engineers or ops teams to stretch feasibility thinking.
