# Assumption Map

*Decide · 45 min · Discovery, Prioritisation, Understanding the problem*

## Purpose

Prioritise assumptions based on how risky and uncertain they are. Focuses energy on testing what matters most.

## When to Use

- After Assumption Collecting
- When planning experiments or MVPs
- To identify what to validate first

## How to Run

**Duration:** 45 minutes

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes

**Steps:**

1. Draw a grid with Certainty (known ↔ unknown) on one axis and Risk (low ↔ high) on the other.
2. Take the team's assumptions and place them on the grid.
3. Discuss:
    - How bad would it be if we were wrong?
    - How much do we really know about this?
4. Identify the riskiest, most uncertain assumptions.
5. Turn those into Hypothesis Statements for validation.

## Tips

- Revisit regularly as assumptions shift from "unknown" to "known"
- Use colour coding to distinguish user vs business vs technical assumptions
- Focus testing on the top-right quadrant (high risk, high uncertainty)
