# Session Principles

*Techniques · 5 min · Alignment*

## Purpose

Agree as a group on principles for how the session will run. Helps prevent poor habits like side conversations or disengagement.

## When to Use

- At the beginning of a workshop
- With groups that haven't worked together much
- When you want to set clear behavioural expectations

## How to Run

**Duration:** 5-10 minutes

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace

**Steps:**

1. Write "Principles" at the top of your workspace.
2. Ask participants to suggest behaviours that will make the session effective (e.g., "No phones," "Listen more than you talk").
3. Capture and briefly clarify each one.
4. Remind the group they can pause to update or add principles if needed.
5. At the end, review: Are these still serving us? Do we want to adapt them?

## Tips

- Encourage the group to self-manage — don't let the facilitator be the only enforcer
- Keep principles short and memorable
- Common examples: one conversation at a time, phones away, build on ideas
