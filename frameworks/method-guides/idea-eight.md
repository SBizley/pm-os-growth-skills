# Idea Eight

*Ideas · 30 min · Ideation*

## Purpose

Force fast, divergent thinking by asking people to generate eight ideas in eight minutes. Restriction unlocks creativity.

## When to Use

- When you want a big volume of ideas quickly
- To push past "first obvious answers"
- Before refining and evaluating

## How to Run

**Duration:** 30-35 minutes

**Materials needed:** Paper, markers

**Steps:**

1. Ask participants to fold paper into eight boxes (or create an 8-box digital grid).
2. Clarify the problem to solve (use a How Might We if available).
3. Set a timer: one minute per box. Everyone writes or sketches one idea per minute.
4. After eight minutes, have participants share their grids with the group.
5. Encourage "idea stealing" - remixing others' ideas in later rounds.
6. Optionally, repeat or have participants develop their favourite 2-3 ideas into a T-Bar Format.

## Tips

- The pace will feel uncomfortable - that's the point
- Emphasise that wild ideas are welcome; refinement comes later
- No idea is too small or silly during this exercise
