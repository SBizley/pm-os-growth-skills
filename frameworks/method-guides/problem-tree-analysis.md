# Problem Tree Analysis

*Understand · 1 hr · Understanding the problem*

# **Purpose**

Get beyond symptoms by mapping the **causes** and **effects** of a problem to identify what truly drives it.

# **When to Use**

- When a problem feels complex or multi-layered
- To differentiate symptoms from root causes
- Before deciding which part of the system to address first

# **How to Run**

**Duration:** 1 hour

**Materials:** Whiteboard or Miro

**Steps:**

1. Write your **core problem** in the centre.
2. Draw **branches upward** for effects or visible symptoms.
3. Draw **roots downward** for possible causes.
4. For each root, ask “why” repeatedly to dig deeper.
5. Identify causes you can directly influence vs. external dependencies.

# **Example**

Problem: “Onboarding takes too long.”

- **Effects:** Delayed activation, support frustration.
- **Causes:** Manual ID checks, unclear email comms, missing automation.
    
    **Root cause:** Dependency on manual ops verification.
    

# **Tips**

- Don’t stop too early - keep asking “why.”
- Colour-code effects (red) and causes (blue).
- Revisit once discovery adds new data.
