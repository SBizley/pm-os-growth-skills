# Democratic Discussion

*Discuss · 1 hr · Alignment*

## Purpose

Run a fair and efficient group discussion by co-creating the agenda and timeboxing topics. Ensures the most important issues are heard.

## When to Use

- When there are too many topics and not enough time
- In team meetings where everyone needs a voice
- To prevent loud voices from dominating

## How to Run

**Duration:** 1 hour

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes, timer

**Steps:**

1. Draw three columns: **To Discuss, Discussing, Discussed**.
2. Ask participants to write topics they want to cover and place them in "To Discuss."
3. Have each person briefly explain their topic.
4. Hold a Secret Vote to decide which topics are most important.
5. Arrange topics in order of votes.
6. Start with the highest-ranked topic. Timebox each to 10 minutes.
7. At the end of each slot, decide to continue for another 10 minutes or move on.
8. Capture actions from each topic in Who/What/When.

## Tips

- Works well with large groups to prevent rabbit holes
- Helps ensure quieter voices shape the agenda
- Be strict about timeboxing to maintain momentum
