# OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)

*Goals · 1 hr · Alignment, Kickoff, Ready to decide*

## Purpose

Give the team radical focus by setting one clear objective and linking it to measurable results.

## When to Use

- At the start of a quarter or project
- When you need a tight, shared focus
- To align team effort around a single ambitious goal

## How to Run

**Duration:** 1 hour

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes

**Steps:**

1. Before the workshop, ask participants to submit their suggested objectives. Each should be a qualitative, aspirational end-state (e.g., "Launch the most reliable beta version").
2. Share all objectives and cluster similar ones.
3. Discuss and vote to select one primary objective.
4. Brainstorm potential metrics that would show progress toward this objective.
5. Narrow down to three metrics and turn them into measurable Key Results (e.g., "Get 40 pre-orders per month").
6. Ensure the Key Results feel like a stretch — achievable, but not guaranteed.

## Tips

- Avoid setting multiple objectives: it dilutes focus
- Revisit OKRs regularly (weekly or bi-weekly) to track progress
- Key Results should be quantitative, not qualitative
- Aim for 70% achievement — if you hit 100%, you aimed too low
