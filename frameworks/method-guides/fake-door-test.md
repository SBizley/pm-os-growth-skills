# Fake Door Test

*Evaluate · 2 hrs · Validation Solution*

## Purpose
Measure real customer interest in a new feature or product before it exists.

## When to Use

- Early in solution validation
- To compare demand between ideas

## How to Run
**Duration:** 1-3 days

1. Add a "coming soon" CTA or button in-app, email, or web.
2. Track clicks, sign-ups, or survey completions.
3. Share "thanks, we're exploring this" message afterward.
4. Compare engagement to baseline features.

## Example
A "Get a spot when someone cancels" button on full classes drew four times more clicks than a "Join waitlist" button. *(Fictional example.)*

## Tips
- Run on real traffic, not internal tests.
- Follow up with a short feedback form for colour.
