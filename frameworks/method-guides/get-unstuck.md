# Get Unstuck

*Recipe · 3-4 hrs · Ideation, Problem-Solving*

## Purpose

Diagnose why a project or team feels blocked and generate viable options to move forward.

## When to Use

- When progress has stalled and no one knows why
- When the team is circling around the same blocker
- When frustration is high but clarity is low

## How to Run

**Duration:** 3-4 hours

**Materials needed:** Whiteboard or digital workspace, sticky notes, markers

**Steps:**

1. **Sailboat (1 hr)** - Map goals, drivers, and blockers. Identify the biggest anchor.
2. **Five Whys (30-60 min)** - Dig into the root cause of that blocker.
3. **How Might We… (1 hr)** - Reframe the blocker as an open problem.
4. **Reverse Brainstorm (1 hr)** - Generate "bad ideas" to make the problem worse, then flip them into real solutions.
5. **Rose, Thorn, Bud (30 min)** - Stress-test the potential solutions.

## Tips

- Start broad, then narrow to one concrete problem
- End with commitments using Who/What/When so the team leaves with action
- Create a safe space for honesty about what's really blocking progress

## Outcome

A deeper understanding of why the team is stuck and clear options to move forward
