## [Example] Test waitlist auto-fill before building fees or a full feature
- **Decision:** Run a four-week pilot of waitlist auto-fill with five
  studios before committing to a full build. No cancellation fees for now.
- **Context:** Founder asked for no-show fees. Discovery found late
  cancellations were the real revenue loss, and owners didn't want to
  charge regulars.
- **Options we considered:** (1) build fees, (2) build full auto-fill
  with smart waitlist ranking, (3) pilot simple auto-fill with five
  studios, (4) do nothing.
- **Why this one:** Option 3 tests the riskiest assumption (will people
  take a spot at short notice?) for about two weeks of engineering
  instead of six. We accepted a slower route to a polished feature.
- **What would make us revisit it:** Less than 20% of late-cancelled
  spots re-filled after four weeks, or notification opt-outs up more
  than 2 points.
- **Decided by:** Founder, PM · **Consulted:** Eng lead, two studio
  owners
- **Reversible?** Yes, cheaply
