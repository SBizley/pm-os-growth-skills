# Product

_Last updated: example · Fictional company_

## One line
Fernway lets independent fitness studios run their timetable, bookings
and payments in one place, without paying for software built for gym
chains.

## The problem we solve
"I spend Sunday night juggling three apps and still find out on Tuesday
that a class was double-booked." (studio owner, interview)

## Who it's for (and who it isn't)
- **For:** independent studios, 3 to 10 instructors, class-based
- **Also serves:** instructors who teach across several studios
- **Not for:** big-box gyms and chains (different buyer, different
  sales cycle)

## How it makes money
Monthly subscription per studio plus a small fee on each payment taken
through the app.

## What's live today
| Area | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Timetable | Classes, instructors, capacity | Live |
| Booking | Customer app and web booking, waitlists | Live |
| Payments | Packs, memberships, drop-ins | Live |
| Reporting | Basic attendance and revenue | Beta |

## Constraints
Team of 6 engineers. Payments run on a third-party processor, so
anything that changes how money moves needs their sign-off.
